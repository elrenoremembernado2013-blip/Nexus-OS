# NEXUS OS Editions & Desktop Architecture Decision (Phase 12–13.0)

## Edition Separation & Phase 13.0 Readiness Summary

All four NEXUS OS editions share the **Linux Mint 22.3 / Ubuntu 24.04 Noble (`x86_64`)** foundation (`ufw`, `unattended-upgrades`, signed APT repos, `NetworkManager`, `shim-signed`, `grub-efi-amd64-signed`, `mokutil`, `ntfs-3g`, `os-prober`, `nexus-install-preflight`, `nexus-hw-inventory`, `nexus-hw-diagnostics`, `nexus-hw-qualify`, `nexus-browser-qualify`, `nexus-security-audit`) while keeping configs, manifests, `work-*` build trees, and output ISOs strictly isolated:

1. **NEXUS Standard (Cinnamon)** (`--edition cinnamon`, `linux/config/nexus-standard-cinnamon.conf`, `linux/config/cinnamon-packages.list`) -> `nexus-os-22.3-cinnamon-amd64.iso` (`3.09 GB`, SHA-256 `a0d633643ba8d08e8856f306c54ccb88fdf16932e487dd199a11f18b95ea345a`). Full desktop identity (`5` themes, dock/compositor effects); `2 GiB` `Development` floor (`llvmpipe` latency caveat), `4 GiB` `Alpha` recommended; physical hardware & physical GPU browser bench `NOT TESTED`.
2. **MADOO Xfce Baseline** (`--edition xfce`, `linux/config/nexus-system.conf`, `linux/config/mint-packages.list`) -> `nexus-os-22.3-xfce-amd64.iso` (`3.03 GB`, SHA-256 `1630de9eaba1196386f628c4d25bc09eea55643ef40521edcdc5ee9645ca5c3b`). `Alpha` (`4 GiB` QEMU validated; MADOO Chromebook SOF/hardware qualification `NOT TESTED` on physical device).
3. **NEXUS Lite — LXQt Primary (`>= 1 GiB` Lite)** (`--edition lite-lxqt`, `linux/lite/nexus-lite-lxqt.conf`, `linux/lite/manifests/lite-lxqt-packages.list`) -> `nexus-os-22.3-lite-lxqt-amd64.iso` (`2.87 GB`, SHA-256 `05970e975a37d3141ad786b46e8d22dffd82aab124de7e90028fc904934daa89`). `Alpha` at `>= 1 GiB` (`508 MiB` live idle / `562 MiB` installed idle at `1 GiB`; passed Phase 12.5 manual partitioning, interrupted-install recovery, LUKS2 cold-boot unlock + persistence, Firefox `6`-tab QEMU benchmarks at `1G/2G/4G`, and Phase 13.0 dual-boot `--keep-efi` + enforced OVMF Secure Boot). `512 MiB` remains `Experimental`. Physical hardware `NOT TESTED`.
4. **NEXUS Lite — Openbox Ultra-Minimal (`512–768 MiB` Fallback)** (`--edition lite`, `linux/lite/nexus-lite.conf`, `linux/lite/lite-packages.list`) -> `nexus-os-22.3-lite-amd64.iso` (`2.84 GB`, SHA-256 `6417381b5a9bc8aa027b112df7ee63482a2460fd34fef81f78ddd46a21a14fab`). Preserved intact for ultra-low-memory hardware (`237 MiB` live idle at `512 MiB`, `364 MiB` at `768 MiB`); `512 MiB` remains `Experimental`, `768 MiB` `Development`, `>= 1 GiB` `Alpha`. Physical hardware `NOT TESTED`.
